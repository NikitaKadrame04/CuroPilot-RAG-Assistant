What is the problem?
When emergencies happen, most hospitals start from zero.
Every minute matters — yet hospitals receive patients with no prior information about injuries, vitals, or severity.
Hospitals receive patients with zero prior information.
Doctors have no idea about injuries, vitals, or trauma level. They start from scratch the moment the stretcher enters — wasting minutes that decide survival.
Diagnosis and treatment are delayed.
Gathering doctors, preparing equipment, readying the OT, and calling specialists — all begins after arrival instead of before.
Ambulance–hospital communication is broken.
Phone calls are rushed, incomplete, and lack structure. There is no standardised system for transmitting critical patient data in real time.
The Golden Hour is lost.
The first 60 minutes after major trauma decide survival. When hospitals start from zero, those minutes slip away while teams scramble.
The reality
Hospitals begin treatment setup only after the ambulance arrives — losing 8–20 critical minutes.
